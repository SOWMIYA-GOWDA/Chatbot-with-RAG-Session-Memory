# Company Leave Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement
Full-time employees are entitled to 18 paid annual leave days per calendar year.
Annual leave can be used for vacation, personal work, or family commitments.
Contractors are not eligible for paid annual leave.

## 2. Sick Leave
All employees are entitled to 10 sick leave days per year.
Sick leave can be used for personal illness, medical treatment, or hospital visits.
Medical proof may be required if sick leave exceeds 2 consecutive days.

## 3. Carry-Over / Rollover Rules
Employees can carry forward up to 5 unused annual leave days to the next year.
Any unused leave beyond 5 days will expire automatically.

## 4. Leave Approval Process
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal.
Employees should apply at least 3 working days in advance.
Manager approval is mandatory before taking leave.
Emergency leave must be informed immediately.

## 5. Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave is allowed if paid leave balance is exhausted.
Unpaid leave requires manager approval.
Salary deduction will apply for unpaid leave days.

## 6. Contractors vs Full-Time Employees
Full-time employees receive paid annual leave and sick leave.
Contractors are eligible only for unpaid leave.
Contractors must inform their reporting manager before taking leave.

## 7. Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to 12 company-declared public holidays per year.
Public holidays do not count as annual leave.

## 8. Maternity and Special Leave
Female employees are eligible for maternity leave as per government rules.
Special leave may be granted for emergencies with HR approval.
